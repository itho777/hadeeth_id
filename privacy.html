Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Welcome to HADEETH.ID ("the Platform", "we", "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our website at hadeeth.id and its subdomains. By using HADEETH.ID, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Who We Are

HADEETH.ID is a non-commercial, educational Hadith reference platform designed to facilitate access to authenticated Islamic hadith literature. The platform is operated independently and is not affiliated with any commercial organisation.

2. Information We Collect

We collect minimal information. Specifically:

  • Browser localStorage: We store your reading preferences (last-read hadith, language preference, dark/light mode, and Hadith of the Day setting) locally in your browser. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us.
  • Usage Analytics: We may use standard web analytics tools (such as Cloudflare Web Analytics) that collect anonymised, aggregate traffic data including page views, general geographic region, and device type. No personally identifiable information (PII) is collected.
  • Search Queries: If you use the search feature powered by Algolia or Cloudflare AI, your search terms may be transmitted to those third-party services for processing. Please review their respective privacy policies.

3. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect your name, email address, or any contact information unless you contact us directly.
  • We do not use tracking cookies for advertising purposes.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share any user data with third parties for marketing purposes.
  • We do not require account registration to use any part of the platform.
