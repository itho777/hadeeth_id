Detailed Biography & Historical Accounts

Key life events, scholarly journey, and famous traditions.

Early Life & Conversion: Born in Makkah into the Banu 'Adi clan of Quraysh, 'Umar bin Al-Khattab was initially a vocal opponent of Islam until his historic conversion in the 6th year of Prophethood. His acceptance of Islam gave public strength to the early Muslim community.

Role in Transmission: Although 'Umar was careful to avoid unnecessary narration lest people distract themselves from the Quran, his transmitted Hadiths carry immense weight because they directly concern governance, jurisprudence, intent, and judicial principles.

The Narration of Intention: When 'Umar delivered the famous sermon on the Minbar of Madinah stating "Actions are judged by intentions", it was recorded by 'Alqama ibn Waqqas and became the founding narration of Islamic ethics across all six canonical books.
